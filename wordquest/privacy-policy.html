Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Quest (the “App”), published under the Obsidixz name, handles information. It applies to the App and its related game services. Word Quest does not require you to create a separate Word Quest account.

Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay and preference information in its private app storage. This can include puzzle progress, settings, coin balance and transaction history, booster inventory, streak or daily-play state, and other information needed to resume play. This information remains on your device unless you clear the App’s storage or remove the App. Uninstalling or clearing storage may remove local progress.

Google Play Games and saved games

If Google Play Games sign-in and Saved Games are available, and you sign in, the App may use Google Play Games Services to identify your game profile and store or retrieve a game-save snapshot associated with that profile. The snapshot can contain the gameplay and economy information described above. Sign-in and cloud-save availability are controlled by Google and may vary; cloud synchronization is not guaranteed. You may continue with local play when those services are unavailable.

Google processes information for these services under its own terms and privacy disclosures. The App does not receive your Google password.

Purchases

Purchases offered in the App are processed by Google Play Billing. The App receives product and transaction information needed to complete a purchase, grant the selected in-game item, and prevent duplicate grants. The App does not receive your payment-card number or other full payment credentials; Google Play handles payment details. Purchase-related grant records may be kept with local game data and, where a saved-game feature is available, may be included in the game-save information. Google Play controls payment processing, purchase history, and applicable refund handling.

Gameplay analytics

When analytics is enabled for a build and the required owner configuration is present, the App sends limited gameplay events to GameAnalytics. Events can describe puzzle progression, virtual-currency gains or spending, booster or hint use, theme selection, and aggregate session activity. The App is designed to send fixed event names and limited numeric or category values, not the words you enter or free-form messages. GameAnalytics may process a device or other identifier for analytics. The owner-approved Play Data Safety description for this processing is: “Device or other IDs — collected, for Analytics; not shared for advertising; data encrypted in transit.” GameAnalytics processes the events as an analytics service provider under its own privacy terms.

Network requests and service providers

The App may make HTTPS requests to download a small public configuration file hosted on GitHub Pages and to check network time using Google or Cloudflare endpoints. These requests do not include your game-save contents. As with ordinary internet connections, the relevant service providers may receive network information such as your IP address and request metadata. Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, and GameAnalytics (when analytics is enabled) process information under their respective privacy terms.

Advertising

In the app configuration reviewed for this draft, the LevelPlay advertising startup setting is disabled, so that configuration does not initialize the ad SDK or make ad requests. Advertising is not used for analytics. Before enabling ads or releasing a build that serves them, the publisher must revisit this Policy and Google Play’s Data Safety disclosures, including the ad provider, data and identifiers the provider collects or shares, personalization/consent behavior, and the ad formats actually used. Do not rely on this “ads disabled” wording for a build with ads enabled.

Retention and deletion

Local gameplay information remains on your device until you clear the App’s storage or remove the App. These local files are separate from any Google Play Games save. You can manage or delete Google Play Games data for an individual game using Google’s Play Games data controls; see Google’s instructions. Deleting Google Play Games data does not by itself delete a local copy or information held by the App or its service providers.

For a privacy request about information handled by Obsidixz, contact the developer through the support contact shown on the Word Quest Google Play listing. We will review and respond to requests under applicable law, including requests to access, correct, restrict, or erase information where those rights apply. Some information may be retained where permitted or required by law. Analytics-provider retention and deletion are also subject to the provider’s policies and applicable law.

Security

Requests the App makes to its listed network services use HTTPS encryption in transit. Local gameplay data is stored in the App’s private storage area. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and privacy rights

Word Quest is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. The App does not provide in-game fields for entering names, email addresses, or free-form messages. Google Play Games sign-in and, when enabled, analytics may still process profile or device/other identifiers as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the App or its support channels, contact the developer using the support contact on the Word Quest Google Play listing so the request can be reviewed under applicable law. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access to or deletion of information.

Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the App’s features, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published. Material changes will be communicated through the App or its Google Play listing where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer support contact shown on the Word Quest Google Play listing.
